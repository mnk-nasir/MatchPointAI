System Intelligence & AI Logic Guide

This document explains the logic driving the Market Intelligence, Investor Matching, and AI Scoring engines on the platform.

---

1. AI Opportunity Scoring Engine
Goal: Calculates a dynamic Opportunity Score and Risk Score for any startup by blending internal review criteria with live external signals.

Factors weighed:
- Base Score: Derived from internal startup evaluation questions.
- Team Strength: Checks for presence of co-founders; impacts score triggers.
- Sentiment & Buzz: Evaluates continuous news mentions; high sentiment boosts triggers.
- Funding History: Verifies previous capital raising; verified dollars trigger heavy verification.

---

2. Market Intelligence Engine
Goal: Processes high-frequency external signals (Google News feeds, Social Buzz counters) into verifiable dashboards.

Factors weighed:
- News Mentions Score: Counts unique NewsArticle entries tied to startup name in the last 30 days.
- Social Sentiment Score: Extracts sentiment keywords from continuous aggregates.
- Industry Momentum Score: Counts volume of Funding Events inside the exact same industry sector.

---

3. Investor Matching Engine
Goal: Automatically ranks and matches Venture Capital firms to startups to optimize funding success.

Factors weighed (Point Multiplier):
- Industry Alignment: Checks portfolio overlap limits.
- Funding Stage fit: Validates startup stage fit.
- Geography Overlay: Checks continental boundary overlays.
- Check Size target: Inspects target limits within min/max criteria.

---

4. Startup Discovery Agent (The Background Brain)
Goal: Runs automated loops scan external streams to find startups before they are entered manually.

Layers:
- News Scraper: Monitors RSS streams or funding phrases.
- LinkedIn Signal Finder: Tracks founder announcement frameworks.
- Product Launch Finder: Evaluates product launch feeds targeting category buckets.
