AI Opportunity Scoring: Deep Dive

File: backend/core/services/ai_scoring/engine.py

---

1. Core Function: calculate_startup_score(startup)
Computes dynamic Opportunity and Risk scores synthesizing internal form responses against live external social streams.

---

2. Inputs & Dependencies:
- StartupEvaluation Model: Provides foundational total_score variables.
- SocialSignal Model: Supplies live sentiment_score and popularity_score counters.
- FundingEvent Model: Checks for capital verification counters.

---

3. Weighting Logic (0-100 Points):

A. Base Score Setup
- Initial Opp Score = startup.total_score (Default: 50)
- Initial Risk Score = 100 - BaseScore

B. Team Strength Multipliers
- Condition: Checks form JSONB arrays for coFounders.
- Impact: If Co-Founder Exists -> +10 Opp / -5 Risk.

C. News Sentiment & Buzz
- Condition: Filters social streams matching startup name.
- Impact:
  - Strong Sentiment (>80): +15 Opp / -10 Risk.
  - Weak Sentiment (<40): -10 Opp / +15 Risk.
  - Popularity Buzz (>75): +10 Opp.

D. Funding Pipeline Verification
- Condition: Sums linked dollar amounts in Funding tables.
- Impact:
  - > $1,000,000: +15 Opp / -10 Risk.
  - > $0 (Any): +5 Opp.

---

4. Outputs:
Enforces caps ensuring scores stay between 0 and 100.
Derives labels: High (>=80), Medium (>=50), Low (<50).
Directly saves back to startup.opportunity_score and startup.risk_score variables.
