Investor Matching Engine: Deep Dive

File: backend/core/services/investor_matching/engine.py

---

1. Core Function: calculate_investor_matches(startup)
Uses algorithmic score matrix to pair startups with qualified investor candidates strictly based on criteria fit.

---

2. Inputs & Dependencies:
- InvestorProfile Model: Preference criteria (target_industries, preferred_stages, min/max check sizes).
- Startup Attributes: Resolves industry, stage, location, target amounts.

---

3. Score Matrix Logic (100 Points Total):

A. Industry Alignment (Max: 40 Points)
Checks startup sector matching array defaults.
- Strong Align: +40 Points.
- General Tech Alignment: +10 Points.

B. Funding Stage Fit (Max: 30 Points)
- Validates startup lifecycles (Seed, Series A) inside investor preferences array.
- Align: +30 Points.

C. Geography Envelope (Max: 15 Points)
- Confirms continental regional align descriptors boundary overlaps.
- Align: +15 Points.

D. Check Size Target (Max: 15 Points)
- Verifies if startup valuation target fits safely within Investor check sizes limits.
- Align: +15 Points.

---

4. Outputs:
Rejects match aggregates final score falling below 50 Points threshold.
Saves row items directly into StartupInvestorMatch tables storing justified rationale string logs.
Serves rank cards dynamically triggering profile card sidebar mounting loops.
