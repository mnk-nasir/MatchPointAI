Market Intelligence Engine: Deep Dive

File: backend/core/services/market_intelligence/engine.py

---

1. Core Function: calculate_market_intelligence(startup)
Processes high-frequency external signals (Google News and social trackers) creating structured dashboard lookups.

---

2. Inputs & Dependencies:
- NewsArticle Model: Mentions counter populated by background scrapers.
- SocialSignal Model: Live sentiment indexes.
- FundingEvent Model: Sector heatmap indexes verifying industry funding flags.

---

3. Calculation Logic:

A. News Mentions Score
- Logic: Counts article entries linked to startup in last 30 days.
- Formula: Count * 5 (Cap 100).

B. Sentiment Score Tracking
- Logic: Live keyword extraction metrics.
- Formula: Live percentage mapping (0-100). Default fallback is 50.

C. Industry Momentum Score
- Logic: Monitors funding absolute counts strictly flowing into exact industry sector.
- Formula: 40 + (FundingCount * 10) (Cap 100).

---

4. Outputs:
Converts continuous metrics to text tags:
Levels: High (>=75), Medium (>=45), Low (<45).
Trends: Rising (>=70), Stable (>=40), Falling (<40).
Updates the StartupSignal node directly for frontend sidebar mounting.
