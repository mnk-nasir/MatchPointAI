Startup Discovery Agent: Deep Dive

File: backend/core/services/startup_discovery/

---

1. Core Function: agent.py -> run()
Aggregates scraper finds separating stealth startup triggers before manual database creations take place.

---

2. Sub-Collector finders:

A. News Startup Finder (news_startup_finder.py)
- Scans Google News sequences and secondary TechCrunch Aggregators.
- Regex isolates naming sequences sitting prior to launch keywords.
- Extracts round tags and keyword taxonomies dynamically.

B. LinkedIn Signal Finder (linkedin_signal_finder.py)
- Monitors triggers announcing stealth deployment frameworks or launch announcements.
- Matches typical name placeholders.

C. Product Launch Finder (product_launch_finder.py)
- Evaluates descriptive launch text separating product categories (SaaS, AI, Marketplace, Hardware, Fintech App).
- Extracts targets accurately.

---

3. De-Duplication & Promote Loops:
- Unique strictly standardizes by (company_name, article_url) node keys.
- Discards invalid variables (strings > 80 lengths or noise triggers).
- Promotes row creation triggering StartupEvaluation creating baseline row assigning stage triggers.
Leaves descriptors safely stored continuous inner loop JSON arrays.
